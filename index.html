02研究兴趣Research interests

我关注 AI 参与信息获取和决策后，人们如何形成判断、作出选择，以及评价决策者的责任。当前研究围绕以下四个方向展开。

I am interested in how people form judgments, make choices, and evaluate decision-makers' responsibility when AI participates in information seeking and decision-making. My current research focuses on four areas.

群体智慧

Collective intelligence

梳理群体智慧研究的理论来源、核心问题与评价方法，区分个体判断的聚合、小组跨任务表现和协作系统的组织方式。在此基础上，关注 AI 参与的人类群体与大语言模型多智能体系统，比较不同研究的任务、互动方式和表现标准。

I review the theoretical foundations, central questions, and evaluation methods of collective intelligence research, distinguishing the aggregation of individual judgments, group performance across tasks, and the organization of collaborative systems. I also examine research on human groups involving AI and on LLM multi-agent systems, comparing their tasks, interaction patterns, and performance criteria.

信息获取与群体意见形成

Information acquisition and group opinion formation

关注使用大语言模型获取信息如何改变群体成员接触和分享的信息，以及这些变化如何通过个体意见更新影响群体意见分布。

I examine how using large language models to obtain information changes what group members encounter and share, and how these changes affect group opinion distributions through individual opinion updating.

人类与 AI 的错误评价和选择

Evaluating errors and choosing between humans and AI

关注人们如何比较人类与 AI 的表现、评价其错误，并在两者之间作出选择；区分对表现的判断、错误后果与来源偏好在选择中的作用。

I study how people compare human and AI performance, evaluate their errors, and choose between them, distinguishing the roles of performance beliefs, error consequences, and source preferences.

AI 决策代理、冲突行为与责任判断

AI decision agents, conflict behavior, and responsibility judgments

关注允许将最终决策交由 AI 作出时，人们的冲突选择是否改变，以及结果发生后，受益者和受损者如何分别评价人类决策者与 AI 的责任。

I investigate whether allowing people to delegate final decisions to AI changes conflict choices, and how those who benefit or are harmed subsequently evaluate the responsibility of the human decision-maker and the AI.

研究方法以行为实验、基于主体的仿真和文献梳理为主。

My methodological interests include behavioral experiments, agent-based modeling, and literature reviews.
